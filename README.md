### 📌 Case Study Overview
**GokulaHealth** is a dairy herd and milk production management platform designed for small-to-medium dairy farmers. 

The project traces a full product evolution across two major phases:
* **Existing V1 Product**: An initial Android application designed for offline cattle logging and basic milk recording.
* **V2 Product Workflow**: A redesigned, structured product lifecycle aimed at streamlining daily milk entry, providing instant input validation, automating monthly yield analytics, and establishing formal Agile Scrum execution.

## 🛠️ Skills & Competencies Demonstrated

### Product Management (PM)
* **Problem Discovery & User Research**: Conducting farmer interviews, identifying field pain points, and synthesizing insights into problem briefs.
* **PRD & Specs**: Authoring Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) with clear objectives, technical constraints, user personas, and success metrics.
* **User Stories & Acceptance Criteria**: Writing granular user stories with Gherkin-style Acceptance Criteria (`Given-When-Then`).
* **UX Prototyping**: Designing wireframes and high-fidelity interaction flows in Figma.
* **Product Analytics & Validation**: Planning post-launch feedback loops, user sentiment reports, and data-driven iteration plans.

### Project Management & Product Operations (PJM / Product Ops)
* **Agile & Scrum Execution**: Configuring Jira Cloud workspaces, defining Epics, building prioritized backlogs, and managing Sprint 1 execution.
* **Prioritization Frameworks**: Applying **MoSCoW** and **RICE** methodologies to balance developer effort against user impact.
* **Roadmapping**: Structuring quarterly and version-based strategic product roadmaps.
* **Quality Assurance (QA)**: Structuring formal QA test plans, functional verification matrices, and edge case scenario tests.
* **Release Operations**: Drafting structured Release Notes, deployment checklists, and rollout communication strategies.
